Reversim Summit 2025 (RS25) is a community driven event in Israel for software developers, product managers, data scientists and everyone else in the software industry. With an enthusiastic team of volunteers we've been creating a content-first software conferences since 2013 and successfully leading the market. We are grateful to have such an excellent top of the industry team of volunteer moderators and event organizers.

Start.io

Start.io is a sell-side omnichannel advertising platform powered by real-time mobile audiences. We deliver hundreds of millions of ads per day across thousands of global leading apps. Our platform uses AI to deliver more efficient, effective and precise digital advertising campaigns. Our direct integration with thousands of mobile publishers gives us access to more than 50 billion first-party data signals per day across the globe. Marketers use these anonymized signals to understand and predict consumer behavior, identify new opportunities and fuel business growth. Start.io was launched in 2010 by cofounders Gil Dudkiewicz and Ran Avidan, and today has offices in New York, Tel Aviv, Beijing, the U.K., Ukraine, Poland, and India.

Fullpath

Fullpath was founded in 2014 by three friends who came together to build a Customer Data Platform (CDP) and an AI-driven marketing automation platform (MAP) that now serve thousands of North American car dealerships, including some of the most savvy and forward leading auto-retailers in the United States. Fullpath is driven by a passion for the impact that small, medium, and family-owned businesses have on local communities and economies. This has guided our mission since 2017 when our team wrote the company’s first piece of dealer-focused software. We’re still at it every day since and are gratefully learning from our customers and partners as we go.

Minimus

Minimus’ secure container images reduce vulnerabilities by over 97% compared to typical container images. Images are continuously rebuilt from source, containing only the necessary bits to run the application, radically reducing the attack surface. Minimus images are OCI compliant and deployed with a single line change in configuration files. In addition to hardened images, Minimus provides real-time exploit advisories, provable compliance with NIST, CIS and STIG benchmarks, and no-code actions and integrations that eliminate time consuming remediation work and deliver instant risk reduction to dev and security teams. Founded in 2022 by the team behind container security pioneer Twistlock and the author of NIST SP 800-190, Minimus has raised a $51 million seed round from YL Ventures and Mayfield. The company is headquartered in Baton Rouge with offices in New York, Tel Aviv, and Portland, OR.
